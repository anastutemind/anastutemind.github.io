PalmScan — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 August 2026

PalmScan ("the app") is developed and published by Adnan Abbasi ("we", "us"). This policy explains what data the app handles.

Camera

PalmScan uses your device camera to detect and analyse your palm. All processing happens entirely on your device using on-device machine learning (MediaPipe). Camera frames are never uploaded, transmitted, or shared — the app has no server and gameplay requires no internet connection.

Photos

When you capture a reading, the resulting image is saved to your device's photo gallery (Pictures/PalmScan) so you can keep or share it. These photos stay on your device under your control; we never receive them. Debug data exports (if you use the long-press option) are likewise saved only to your device.

Data we collect

None. The app requires no account and collects no personal data, analytics, or identifiers.

Entertainment disclaimer

Palm readings generated by the app are for entertainment purposes only and do not constitute advice of any kind.

Children

No data is collected from any user, including children.
