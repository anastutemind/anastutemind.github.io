PalmScan Privacy Policy

App: PalmScan (Android package com.anaskhan.palmscan) · Developer: Anas Khan · Last updated: 5 November 2026

PalmScan reads a palm from your camera and draws the reading on your own device. You don't need an account, and no photo or reading is uploaded anywhere. This policy explains exactly what the app does with your camera.

Data we collect

We don't collect, store or sell any personal information. The app has no sign-up, no login, no analytics of its own and no advertising SDK, and it does not transmit your photos or readings to any server.

Camera

  • Camera frames are processed entirely on your device to locate the hand, rectify the palm and trace its lines.
  • Frames are processed in memory and discarded as you scan. They are not uploaded, not stored in the cloud and not visible to us.
  • The camera is used only while you are on the scanning screen and only after you have granted the permission. You can revoke it at any time in Android's settings.

Photos you choose to keep

If you tap save, the annotated palm image is written to your own gallery through Android's media store. It stays on your device, under your control, and is never sent to us.

Advertising and tracking

The app contains no ads, no advertising ID use, no tracking SDKs and no third-party analytics.

Internet

The app works fully offline. All hand tracking, line tracing and the reading itself are computed on your phone.

Entertainment only

Palm readings are provided for entertainment. They are not medical, psychological, financial or any other kind of professional advice, and they are not a prediction of your future.

Children

PalmScan is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children. Because nothing is collected at all, there is nothing to expose.

Security

Because we don't hold your personal data and nothing leaves your device, there is nothing of yours on our side to breach.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the "Last updated" date above.
