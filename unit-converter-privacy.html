Unit Converter — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 September 2026

Unit Converter ("the app") is developed and published by Adnan Abbasi ("we", "us"). This policy explains what data the app handles.

Data we collect

None. Unit Converter does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. The app requires no account, no sign-in, and has no internet permission.

Data stored on your device

Your last-used values, selected units, converter mode, and any units you pin as favourites are saved only in the app's private storage on your device. This data never leaves your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

Advertising and analytics

The app shows no advertisements and includes no analytics, tracking, or third-party SDKs.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and, as no data is collected, no personal information of children is processed.
