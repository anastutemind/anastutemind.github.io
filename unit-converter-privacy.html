Unit Converter Privacy Policy

App: Unit Converter (Android package com.anaskhan.unitconverter) · Developer: Anas Khan · Last updated: 5 November 2026

Unit Converter converts between units of length, weight, temperature, volume, speed, area and more. You don't need an account to use it, and we (the developer) don't run any server that collects your personal data. This policy explains what the app stores and what it does not.

Data we collect

We don't collect, store or sell any personal information. The app has no sign-up, no login, no analytics of its own and no advertising SDK.

Data stored on your device

  • Preferences such as the units you last used, favourite conversions and display settings are saved locally on your device so the app opens the way you left it.
  • Conversion history, if you use it, is kept on your device only.

This data never leaves your device. Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app deletes it.

Advertising and tracking

The app contains no ads, no advertising ID use, no tracking SDKs and no third-party analytics. It does not profile you and it does not share data with anyone.

Internet

All conversions are calculated on your device. The app does not upload your data and does not require an internet connection to work.

Permissions

Unit Converter does not ask for access to your contacts, camera, microphone, photos, files or precise location.

Children

Unit Converter is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children. Because nothing is collected at all, there is nothing to expose.

Security

Because we don't hold your personal data, there is nothing of yours on our side to breach. Anything the app stores lives only on your own device, protected by Android's app sandbox.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the "Last updated" date above.
