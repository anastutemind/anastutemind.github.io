Unit Converter — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 September 2026

Unit Converter ("the app") is developed and published by Adnan Abbasi ("we", "us"). This policy explains what data the app handles.

Data we collect

We do not require an account and we do not run our own analytics. The app itself does not send your conversions or favourites to us.

Data stored on your device

Your last-used values, selected units, converter mode, and any units you pin as favourites are saved only in the app's private storage on your device. This data is deleted when you uninstall the app.

Advertising

The app shows banner ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect device identifiers (including the advertising ID), IP address, and approximate location in order to serve and measure ads. That data is handled by Google under the Google Privacy Policy and AdMob policies.

In the European Economic Area, UK and Switzerland a consent form is shown before personalised ads. You can reset advertising ID in Android settings.

Internet

The converter works on-device. Internet is used only to load ads.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
