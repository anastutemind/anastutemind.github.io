Mushaf — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 September 2026

Mushaf ("the app") is developed and published by Adnan Abbasi ("we", "us"). This policy explains what data the app handles.

Data we collect

None. We do not require an account, and we do not collect, store, or share personal data, analytics, or advertising identifiers. There is no Mushaf server operated by us.

Data stored on your device

Reading preferences and, if you choose to use optional cloud voice, a voice API key you paste yourself, are saved in the app's private storage. Audio you play may also be cached on the device for offline reuse. This data never leaves your device except as described below, and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

Third-party content (Quran text and audio)

To display the Quran and play recitation, the app requests public content from Quran.com's CDN (api.qurancdn.com, audio.qurancdn.com) and, for some translations, everyayah.com. Those providers receive a normal web request (including your IP address) in order to deliver the files. We do not receive that traffic.

The app may also load fonts from Google Fonts when a screen uses them.

Optional cloud voice

On-device text-to-speech runs locally and sends nothing to us. If you optionally enter your own Fish Audio API key, verse text you ask to hear is sent to Fish Audio (api.fish.audio) using your key so they can generate audio. Do not use that option if you do not want text sent to Fish Audio. Their practices are governed by Fish Audio's own privacy policy.

Advertising

The app shows no advertisements.

Children

The app may be used by all ages. We collect no personal information from any user, including children.
