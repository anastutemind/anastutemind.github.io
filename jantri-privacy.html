Jantri Privacy Policy

App: Jantri (Android package com.anaskhan.jantri) · Developer: Anas Khan · Last updated: 5 November 2026

Jantri calculates Ramadan sehri and iftar times for your location and reminds you before they arrive. Everything is computed on your phone. There is no account, no server and no analytics.

Data we collect

We don't collect, store or sell any personal information. The app has no sign-up, no login, no analytics of its own and no advertising SDK, and it does not transmit your settings, your location or your usage to any server.

Location

  • If you tap "Use my current location", the app asks Android for a single coarse or precise fix and uses it only to compute prayer times for those coordinates.
  • The coordinates are stored in the app's own settings on your device. They are never uploaded.
  • You don't have to grant location at all: you can pick your city from a list bundled with the app, or type coordinates by hand.

Notifications and alarms

Sehri and iftar reminders are scheduled locally with Android's alarm manager and posted by the app itself. On Android 13 and newer the app asks for notification permission, and on Android 12 and newer it asks you to allow exact alarms so a reminder is not delayed. No notification content leaves the device.

Advertising and tracking

The app contains no ads, no advertising ID use, no tracking SDKs and no third-party analytics.

Internet

The app works fully offline. The city list ships inside the app and the sun position is computed arithmetically on your phone.

Bundled data

City names and coordinates are derived from the GeoNames database, used under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 licence.

Religious accuracy

Times are astronomical calculations. Local moon sighting decides the start of Ramadan and the day of Eid, and mosques differ in the twilight angle and safety margins they use; the app exposes both so you can match your masjid's printed timetable. Follow your masjid's announcement where it differs.

Children

Jantri is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children. Because nothing is collected at all, there is nothing to expose.

Security

Because we don't hold your personal data and nothing leaves your device, there is nothing of yours on our side to breach.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the "Last updated" date above.
