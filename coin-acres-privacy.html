Coin Acres Privacy Policy

App: Coin Acres (Android package com.anaskhan.coinacres) · Developer: Anas Khan · Last updated: 5 November 2026

Coin Acres is a single-player farming game. You don't need an account to play it, and we (the developer) don't run any server that collects your personal data. This policy explains what the game stores and what it does not.

Data we collect

We don't collect, store or sell any personal information. The game has no sign-up, no login, no analytics of its own and no advertising SDK.

Data stored on your device

  • Save game data (coins, buildings, upgrades, settings) is written to the app's private storage on your device so you can carry on where you left off. It is saved automatically and periodically while you play.
  • Sound and graphics settings are stored the same way.

This data never leaves your device. Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the game deletes it.

Advertising and tracking

The game contains no ads, no advertising ID use, no tracking SDKs and no third-party analytics. It does not profile you and it does not share data with anyone.

Internet

The game is fully playable offline. It does not upload your progress, and there is no cloud save.

Permissions

Coin Acres does not ask for access to your contacts, camera, microphone, photos, files or precise location.

Children

Coin Acres is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children. Because nothing is collected at all, there is nothing for a child's account to expose.

Security

Because we don't hold your personal data, there is nothing of yours on our side to breach. Your save file lives only on your own device, protected by Android's app sandbox.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the "Last updated" date above.
