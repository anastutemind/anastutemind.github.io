Coin Acres — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 August 2026

Coin Acres ("the game") is developed and published by Adnan Abbasi ("we", "us"). This policy explains what data the game handles.

Data we collect

None. Coin Acres does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. The game requires no account, no sign-in, and no internet permission for gameplay.

Data stored on your device

Your game progress (level, coins, buildings, and audio settings) is saved only in the app's private storage on your device. It never leaves your device and is deleted when you uninstall the game.

Advertising

The current version of Coin Acres shows no advertisements. If a future update introduces optional rewarded advertising, this policy will be updated first, and the ad provider's data practices will be disclosed here and in the Google Play Data Safety section before release.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages and, as no data is collected, no personal information of children is processed.
